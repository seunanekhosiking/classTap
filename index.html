COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers.

Does ClassTap replace SA-SAMS?+

No. SA-SAMS remains the core school system. ClassTap provides simpler classroom workflows connected to it.

Do schools import learners and teachers?+

No. ClassTap uses the school identity, educators, classes and learners already held in SA-SAMS.

What happens when the connection drops?+

Attendance changes are saved on the device and queued for sync. The app clearly shows when the school bridge is offline.

Which devices work today?+

The current app targets Android phones and Windows computers on the school network.

Are marks and subject attendance available?+

Not yet. They remain planned modules while their SA-SAMS structures and school workflows are validated responsibly.
